# About This Garden

The FAC Digital Garden is an early, independently maintained experiment in making Christian learning resources easier to connect and explore. It serves both people who attend FAC and anyone exploring faith.

## Archive versus notes

**Archive entries** describe and link to existing FAC-published material. **Garden notes** are brief editorial reflections that may evolve over time; they do not replace the original source or speak for the church.

## Editorial commitments

- Link to original sources and identify their authors when verified.
- Don't republish full copyrighted articles without permission.
- Check for broken links, privacy issues, and factual/theological accuracy.
- Label exploratory notes as such; refer readers to FAC for official statements.
