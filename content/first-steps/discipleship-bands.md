# Discipleship Bands: Grow with others

FAC's [Discipleship Bands](https://www.faccalgary.com/discipleship) are small groups of three to five people meeting regularly for prayer and honest conversation.

The model emphasizes listening, a safe environment, mutual encouragement, and confidentiality. FAC's page provides more detail on the band questions and suggested practices.
