# Alpha: A place to explore questions

**Wondering who Jesus is or what Christians believe?** [Alpha at FAC](https://www.faccalgary.com/alpha) offers a friendly environment for exploring Christianity, often around a shared meal, video or talk, and discussion.

You can listen or contribute at your own pace. FAC describes Alpha as free and open to honest questions.
