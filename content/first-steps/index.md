# First Steps: Explore Faith at FAC

**Curious about Jesus? New to church? Ready to take another step?**

FAC offers ways to ask questions, learn Christian faith, mark a commitment, and grow alongside others. This guide draws from FAC's [official First Steps pathway](https://www.faccalgary.com/firststeps), while the church website remains the place for current schedules, registration, and pastoral contact.

You do not need to complete all these steps in a prescribed order.

## Find a starting point

### 1. [[first-steps/alpha|Alpha — ask big questions]]
Explore who Jesus is, talk about faith, and listen to others in a welcoming group setting. No prior knowledge required.

### 2. [[first-steps/baptism|Baptism — a public step of faith]]
Find out how FAC describes baptism and how to ask about participating.

### 3. [[first-steps/classes|Classes and Catechism — learn the foundations]]
Learn about Christian belief and practices, including prayer and the Apostles' Creed.

### 4. [[first-steps/discipleship-bands|Discipleship Bands — grow with others]]
Connect with a small group for honest conversation, encouragement, and prayer.
